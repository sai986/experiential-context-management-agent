What this establishes

The empty, learned, transferred and ablated managers share the executor, grading, input cases and compressor. Correct imports retain usable support and status. Legacy imports leave rules inactive. Removing each operation-specific lesson loses its corresponding protection.

Where the current policy fails

An important item tagged as info is always archived. Restoring one of two lost facts leaves the joint task unsuccessful, so binary replay creates no lesson. These failures remain visible in the family summaries.

Inputs and values differ across train, validation and test. Families and authored tag rules are shared. This is a mechanism validation, not proof of semantic or cross-model generalization. Context words are not API costs.
